About

I work as a Research Assistant and PhD candidate at the Chair of Civil Systems Engineering, Technische Universität Berlin, on the IWIQ project - developing BIM-supported automation processes and digital twin systems that integrate greywater recycling and heat recovery into sustainable building design.

I have contributed to two major EU-funded research projects: ASHVIN and REINCARNATE, where I worked on digitalization and circularity in construction. My work included IoT-based data acquisition, deployment of the Mainflux platform, motion sensor integration, and machine-learning based quality assessment approaches, supporting the development of scalable digital twin frameworks for monitoring and improving building performance. In addition to my studies, I have gained several years of professional engineering experience, including materials research at BAM in Germany and roles in Bangladesh involving structural design, construction supervision, project coordination, and sustainable materials applications with UN-IOM and industry partners. That field experience where resources are scarce and every design decision matters shapes how I think about sustainable construction today.

My broader interests span circular economy in the built environment, machine learning for non-destructive testing, life cycle analysis, and the automation of design processes with BIM standards.
